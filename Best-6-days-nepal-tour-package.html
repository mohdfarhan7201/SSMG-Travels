Tour Overview

Experience the religious, natural, and cultural beauty of Nepal with SSMG Travels, Gorakhpur on this special road tour. This 6-day budget-friendly Nepal tour package starts from Gorakhpur and covers major destinations including Lumbini, Pokhara, Kathmandu, and Chitwan. During the journey, visit the birthplace of Lord Buddha in Lumbini, enjoy the breathtaking landscapes of Pokhara, explore Kathmandu's famous temples, and experience the natural beauty of Chitwan. The entire trip is conducted via comfortable Car / AC Bus / Tourist Bus, making it an ideal choice for families, couples, and group travelers.

Tour Highlights:
  • Direct road pickup and drop from Gorakhpur.
  • Visit Nepal’s top religious and tourist destinations (Lumbini, Pokhara, Kathmandu).
  • Safe and comfortable road journey.
  • Most affordable and budget-friendly Nepal tour package.
  • Day-wise Itinerary
  • Inclusions
Day 01: Gorakhpur → Pokhara (via Lumbini)

Morning pickup from Gorakhpur Railway Station / Gorakhpur Airport and drive to Pokhara via the Sonauli border. En route, visit Lumbini – the birthplace of Lord Buddha and the Maya Devi Temple. Continue the journey to Pokhara. Overnight stay in Pokhara.

Day 02: Pokhara Sightseeing

Full day Pokhara sightseeing including Davis Falls, Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave, Bindhyabasini Temple, Seti River Gorge and Phewa Lake. Evening free at Lakeside. Overnight stay in Pokhara.

Day 03: Pokhara → Kathmandu

Morning drive to Kathmandu through scenic mountain roads and river valleys. Arrival and evening free for rest or local market visit. Overnight stay in Kathmandu.

Day 04: Kathmandu Sightseeing

Full day sightseeing covering Pashupatinath Temple, Boudhanath Stupa, Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) and Kathmandu Durbar Square. Overnight stay in Kathmandu.

Day 05: Kathmandu → Chitwan

After breakfast, drive to Chitwan. Arrival and leisure time. Optional jungle activities at Chitwan National Park. Overnight stay in Chitwan.

Day 06: Chitwan → Gorakhpur Departure

Morning return journey to Gorakhpur via Sonauli border. After border formalities, drop-off at Gorakhpur. Tour ends with memorable experiences.
