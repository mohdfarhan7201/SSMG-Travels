Tour Overview

Enjoy a perfect 5-day Nepal road tour with SSMG Travels, Gorakhpur, covering both Kathmandu’s religious and cultural heritage and Pokhara’s natural beauty. This package is ideal for travelers who want to explore Nepal without flight, traveling comfortably by Car / AC Bus / Tourist Bus.

Tour Highlights:
  • Covers both Kathmandu and Pokhara in a single well-planned trip.
  • Visit top sites like Pashupatinath, Boudhanath, and Phewa Lake.
  • Safe and scenic road journey from Gorakhpur via Sonauli border.
  • Perfect for families, couples, and groups seeking a budget-friendly escape.
  • Day-wise Itinerary
  • Inclusions
Day 01: Gorakhpur → Kathmandu

Morning pickup from Gorakhpur Railway Station / Gorakhpur Airport. Enter Nepal via the Sonauli border. After a scenic road journey, arrive in Kathmandu. Hotel check-in and rest. Overnight stay in Kathmandu.

Day 02: Kathmandu Sightseeing

Visit major attractions including Pashupatinath Temple, Boudhanath Stupa, Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) and Kathmandu Durbar Square. Evening free for local market visit. Overnight stay in Kathmandu.

Day 03: Kathmandu → Pokhara

After breakfast, drive to Pokhara. Enjoy beautiful landscapes during the journey. Evening free at Phewa Lake area. Overnight stay in Pokhara.

Day 04: Pokhara Sightseeing

Local sightseeing in Pokhara including Phewa Lake boating, Tal Barahi Temple, Davis Falls, Gupteshwar Mahadev Cave and scenic viewpoints. Evening free at lakeside market. Overnight stay in Pokhara.

Day 05: Pokhara → Gorakhpur Departure

Morning return journey. After completing border formalities, drop-off at Gorakhpur in the evening. Tour ends with beautiful memories.
